Snapter

Race your friends to snap it first

Everyone gets the same random item. First to photograph the real thing wins the round, and an AI judge checks every photo, so screens and printouts don't count.

  • Party with friends in the same room: Classic, Blitz and Teams, with twists and power-ups
  • Creative: one prompt, everyone's best shot, an AI score and the room's vote
  • The Daily: one creative prompt for the whole world, one photo, see where you rank
  • Solo Rush: ten items against the clock, the weekly Gauntlet and your own ghost
  • Levels, quests and a Snap Pass season with rewards for your locker
  • No account, no ads, nothing to buy to play

Optional Host Pass (a subscription) for whoever hosts: more modes (Chain, Sabotage, Imposter, Duel, Tournament and more), every pack, bigger rooms and longer games. Everyone else in the room plays free. Coins, earned by playing or bought, get cosmetics in the shop and Snap Pass Premium.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Questions? Support.
