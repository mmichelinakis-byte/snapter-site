Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap NEW GAME and share the room code. Everyone gets the same item: the first real photo of it wins the round, and an AI judge checks every photo. Alone, play Today's prompt (the Daily) or Solo Rush.

How do I join a friend's game?

Type their room code on Home and tap JOIN, or open the invite link they send you.

How do I restore or cancel the Host Pass?

Restore: Settings → Host Pass → Restore Purchases. Cancel: your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Snapter. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report a photo or a player?

Tap Report under the photo in the reveal or on the scoreboard; you can also block its sender. For anything else, Settings → Report a problem, or email us.

Why does Snapter ask my age?

Snapter's games are for players 13 and over, so the app asks every player's birth month and year the first time it opens. If you entered it wrong, fix it in Settings → Change my age (the first time at once, then once every 7 days). Players under 13 can look around the app but can't play. How we keep it is in the Privacy policy, under Children and age.

How do I delete my data?

Photos are deleted within 24 hours. To delete everything else: Settings → Terms, privacy and safety → Delete my data, or email us and we'll do it for you.
