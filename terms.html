Terms of use

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Playing fair

Snapter is free to play, with no account needed. Your photos stay yours: sending one lets us use it to judge the round and show it to the players in that room, and it's deleted within 24 hours. The AI judge decides each round and can be wrong: any player can challenge a win, and you can report a mistake from Settings. Photograph real things, be kind, and don't try to fool the judge with screens or prints. Photos that break the rules are refused, and repeat offences lead to a ban of 24 hours, 7 days or for good.

Your data

You give a nickname, and an email only if you choose to sign in. Photos are used to judge the round, and each player's last photo of a round is shown to the room during its reveal. Every photo is deleted within 24 hours. We keep anonymous totals (games played, average judging time), your country if you play the daily challenge, and random ids for crews and friends. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold.

Game records

To run the game and keep it safe, we keep gameplay stats: the nicknames you used, games and rounds, photo results, challenges, reports and strikes. Only the Snapter team sees them. Photos are deleted within 24 hours, except a photo that led to a strike or ban (kept for moderators until 30 days after it ends, or as long as a permanent ban stands) and a photo sent with a report about the judge or proved wrong by a challenge (kept up to 90 days to improve the judge). A photo involving a child is never kept, by anyone.

The Host Pass subscription

The Host Pass is an optional auto-renewable subscription (weekly, monthly or yearly); everyone in a room plays free either way. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm, or when the free trial ends if your plan has one. It renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel or change plans anytime in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions; cancelling keeps the Pass until the end of the period you paid for. Coin packs and some cosmetics are one-time purchases, also through Apple. Apple takes every payment and handles refunds (ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com), and we never see your card; a refunded pass, coin pack or cosmetic is taken back. Our server keeps the App Store's transaction number and the purchase's status with your account (or this phone), only to know it's yours on your devices. Codes are stored only as fingerprints.

The Snap Pass

The Snap Pass keeps, with your account or this phone: your season XP and where it came from, your quests, your coins in and out, the cosmetics you own and how you got each one, what you wear (the players in your rooms see it), and your time zone, so daily quests change at your midnight. Coins are earned by playing or bought in coin packs; they have no cash value, can't be cashed out or traded, and are taken back if their purchase is refunded. Snap Pass Premium comes with any Host Pass plan, with a code that includes it, or a week at a time for coins; coins never buy the Host Pass. Delete my data removes all of it.

Safety

Every photo is checked for nudity, violence, hate symbols, drugs and identity documents before anyone sees it. You can report any photo: two reports hide it until a person reviews it, and reports are read within 24 hours. You can block a player from a round's reveal: you won't share a room with them again. There is no free chat, only fixed taunts. Rooms with strangers allow built-in avatars only.

Children

Snapter’s games are for players 13 and over: the app asks every player’s birth month and year, and younger players can look around but can’t play or send photos. Anything harmful to children leads to a permanent ban, and only a fingerprint of the photo is kept, never the photo itself.

Your age

You must be 13 or over to play Snapter. When you first open the app you’re asked your birth month and year: please answer truthfully. If it’s wrong, correct it in Settings → Change my age (once every 7 days). Players under 13 can open the app but can’t play, sign in, add friends, buy anything or send photos. How we use and keep your date of birth is in the Privacy policy, under Children and age.
